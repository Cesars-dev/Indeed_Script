### 2. Ran the stress test — diagnosed account settings redirect

**Result:** `failed — Exceeded max steps` (took 193s, 15 iterations)

**Screenshots revealed:**

| Step | URL | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| step_00 | `www.indeed.com/viewjob` | Job loaded ✅ Account logged in ✅ "Apply with Indeed" button visible ✅ |
| step_01–14 | `secure.indeed.com/settings` | After clicking Apply → redirected to Account Settings instead of SmartApply |

**Settings page showed:**
- Account type: Job seeker ✅
- Email: oswaldin.lara@gmail.com ✅
- Phone: +1 206-881-9903 ✅

**Root cause:** Account has basic info but **no Indeed Resume** — Indeed requires a resume
(uploaded or built) before SmartApply will open. All 10 accounts likely have this issue on
first use.

**Secondary bug:** `page.find("Continue", best_match=True)` was fuzzy-matching something on
the Settings page navigation, causing the script to loop all 15 steps instead of failing fast
on step 1. Script wasted 3 minutes instead of failing in 5s.

---

### 3. Fixed: settings redirect detection in `_complete_application`

Added at the top of the state machine loop (before all other checks):

```python
# === Account incomplete — Indeed redirected to settings instead of SmartApply ===
if "indeed.com" in url and "/settings" in url:
    return {"status": "failed", "reason": "Account redirected to settings — complete Indeed profile manually"}
```

Now fails in ~5s on first detection instead of looping 15 steps × 13s = 3+ minutes.
