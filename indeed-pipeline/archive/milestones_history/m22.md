### 4. Account strategy decision: Gmail friction

**Problem:** Google asks for phone verify / suspicious login when logging into Gmail
accounts from a new AdsPower browser profile + residential proxy.

**Options evaluated:**
- Domain emails → risk of batch-ban if all on same domain
- Multiple domains (2-3) → spreads risk but adds cost/complexity
- **Outlook/Hotmail → best option**: no phone verify, free, different domain from Gmail
- Yahoo → also low friction
- ProtonMail → zero friction but 1 free address only

**Decision:** For new/replacement accounts, use Outlook. Keep existing 9 Gmail accounts
that are already warmed up in AdsPower.
